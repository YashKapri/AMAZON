# AMAZON
Amazon is a global technology company known as the world's largest online retailer and a leader in cloud computing with Amazon Web Services (AWS). Founded in 1994 by Jeff Bezos, Amazon started as an online bookstore and has evolved into a multifaceted enterprise offering e-commerce, digital streaming, AI services, and consumer electronics. It is recognized for innovations in logistics, AI, and robotics, with a mission to be Earth's most customer-centric company. Its services and products span millions of customers worldwide with a strong focus on fast delivery and cloud-based solutions
